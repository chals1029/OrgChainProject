# 🚌 Local Off-Campus Compliance & CHED Workflow

> [!danger] CHED Memorandum Order (CMO) Compliance
> Off-campus activities (field trips, community immersions, sports invitationals, and congresses) require strict compliance with CHED regulations and university safety protocols.

---

## 📋 Off-Campus Mandatory Documents

| Item | Document Name | Template Location | Validation Gate |
| :---: | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1** | **Request for Conduct Form** | `BatStateU-FO-REQ-09_Request-...docx` | OSO & OVCAA Dean approval |
| **2** | **Parent / Guardian Consent (Waiver)** | `BatStateU-FO-SOA-03_Parent_s Consent.doc` | 100% signed waivers before departure |
| **3** | **Matrix of Travel and Tour** | `Copy of Matrix of Travel and Tour.docx` | Route, itinerary, stopovers, driver details |
| **4** | **Matrix of Passengers** | `FORMAT FOR MATRIX OF PASSENGER.docx` | Complete manifest with emergency contacts |
| **5** | **Course Activities Matrix** | `13. Course Activities.docx` | Educational objectives & syllabus alignment |
| **6** | **CHED Compliance Report** | `CHED Compliance Report.docx` | Submitted to regional CHED office |
| **7** | **Certificate of Compliance** | `Certificate of Compliance.docx` | Final university inspection sign-off |

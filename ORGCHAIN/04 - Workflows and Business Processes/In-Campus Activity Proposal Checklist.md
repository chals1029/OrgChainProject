# 📋 In-Campus Activity Proposal Checklist

> [!abstract] Institutional Compliance
> In accordance with the Batangas State University Student Organization Manual, all in-campus events must submit and satisfy the 10 official compliance documents.

---

## 🧾 OrgChain Filing Rules

The current activity form treats the first ten rows above as the core in-campus filing set. It also exposes these conditional requirements when applicable:

- Medical clearance for activities requiring health screening.
- Insurance coverage for travel or physically active events.
- Curriculum vitae for judges, guest speakers, or external resource persons.
- Notarized waiver / parental consent for late-night or weekend activities.
- University facility reservation or permit when a campus facility is used.

The activity form also provides later-upload slots for records that are created during or after an activity. Drafts may be saved without the checklist files, but **Submit for review** requires every active pre-activity upload and creates pending rows in `activity_compliance_docs`.

## 📄 Official document preview and upload

The filing form exposes the official In-Campus templates as file-backed documents. Supported DOCX files can be previewed in the browser, downloaded explicitly, completed in Word or another compatible application, and uploaded through the matching checklist row. The uploaded signed/completed file is the compliance record.

# 📋 In-Campus Activity Proposal Checklist

> [!abstract] Institutional Compliance
> In accordance with the Batangas State University Student Organization Manual, all in-campus events must submit and satisfy the 10 official compliance documents.

---

## 📑 Required Document Checkpoints

| Item # | Document Requirement | Official Template File | Purpose |
| :---: | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **0** | **Checklist Template** | `0. Checklist Template.docx` | Master routing slip and sign-off index |
| **1** | **Activity Proposal Letter** | `Sample Letter.docx` | Formal letter of request addressed to the Dean/Director |
| **2** | **Detailed Programme** | `2. Programme.docx` | Hourly breakdown of activities and speaker list |
| **3** | **Project Proposal** | `3. Project Proposal.docx` | Background, objectives, target participants, and logistics |
| **4** | **Budget Proposal** | `4. Budget Proposal.docx` | Itemized line-item expense budget and revenue sources |
| **5** | **Medical Request Letter** | `5. Medical Request Sample Letter.docx` | Campus clinic coordination and standby medical assistance |
| **6** | **Insurance Request Letter**| `6. Insurance Request Sample Letter.docx`| Group student accident insurance endorsement |
| **7** | **Org Board Resolution** | `7. Resolution of the Organization .docx`| Executive committee approval of the planned activity |
| **8** | **Faculty-In-Charge Form** | `14. Faculty-In-Charge.docx` | Formal assignment and agreement of the faculty adviser |
| **9** | **Waste Policy Form** | `Waste-Policy-Compliance-Form-2026.doc`| Solid waste segregation and eco-friendly compliance plan |
